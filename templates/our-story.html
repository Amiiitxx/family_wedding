Our Story

Where Every Celebration Becomes a Family Story

The journey of Family began in 1994 with a simple vision — to create a shopping experience that understands the needs, traditions and celebrations of every family.

Founded by Imbichi Ahmmad, the business grew with the support and dedication of his family. His vision was carried forward by P.N. Abdul Kader, whose commitment helped shape Family into a trusted name for generations.

The journey began with the opening of the first Family Textiles showroom at Thamarassery. What started as a single showroom gradually grew into a family of destinations serving customers across different locations.

Over the years, Family expanded its presence while continuing to stay connected to the people and communities it serves. Every new location became another opportunity to be part of important moments in the lives of families.

From traditional wedding collections to contemporary fashion, the journey continued to evolve with changing generations, lifestyles and preferences. Today, our collections bring together a wide range of Textile & Apparel choices for weddings, festivals and everyday celebrations.

Our growth has always been guided by one belief: families deserve quality, choice and a shopping experience they can trust.

As we continue to grow, we remain committed to the values that shaped our journey from the beginning — serving families, celebrating traditions and creating memorable experiences together.

From one showroom to a growing family of destinations.
